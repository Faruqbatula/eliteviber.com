Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026. This policy covers the Tidyroll app for iPhone and iPad, made by Eliteviber.

Tidyroll collects no data.

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • No servers, and no third-party code of any kind.
  • Nothing is ever deleted without Apple's own confirmation.

Your photos stay on your device

Tidyroll looks at your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, big videos and Live Photos. All of this happens on your iPhone or iPad, using Apple's built-in tools. Tidyroll has no servers and no code that sends anything over the internet.

To compare photos, Tidyroll uses small versions already stored on your device. Scanning never downloads anything from iCloud.

The only time a photo comes from iCloud is when you ask for it: if you open a photo or video that's stored only in iCloud, or convert a Live Photo that's stored only in iCloud, Apple's Photos framework fetches it from your own iCloud Photos. Tidyroll never uploads anything.

What Tidyroll keeps, and where

Everything Tidyroll saves stays on your device:

  • A photo index: a list of your photos with their dates, sizes and scan results, so Tidyroll doesn't have to start again each time. It isn't included in backups, and it's erased if you take away Tidyroll's photo access.
  • Your choices: what you've kept, what's in the basket, the groups you've reviewed and your progress. Like other app data, these are part of your device backup if you use one (iCloud Backup or a computer), which Apple and you control.
  • A few settings, such as whether to keep scanning while charging.

Deleting Tidyroll deletes all of it.

Deleting and converting

  • Tidyroll never deletes anything by itself. Photos you pick wait in Tidyroll's basket until you choose to delete them.
  • Every delete goes through Apple's confirmation dialog. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can get them back for 30 days.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, deleting a photo removes it from all your devices. If you use an iCloud Shared Library, deleting a shared photo removes it for everyone in it.
  • Turning a Live Photo into a still creates a new photo with the same date, location, Favourite status, albums and edits, then moves the original to Recently Deleted. This also needs Apple's confirmation.

Scanning while charging

If "Keep scanning while charging" is on (it is by default), iOS may let Tidyroll carry on checking new photos in the background while your device is plugged in, often overnight. It never uses the internet, and it stops when you unplug, when the device gets warm, or in Low Power Mode. You can turn it off in Tidyroll's Settings & details.

Sharing your progress

You can share a picture of how much space you've cleared. It's only an image with a number and the Tidyroll name: no photos, links, names, dates or places. It goes to Apple's share sheet, and nothing is sent unless you choose an app to send it with.

No third parties

Tidyroll contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no third-party software development kits. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

If you've chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. That's Apple's feature, and you can turn it off there.

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply to you, never for marketing, and we delete it on request.

You can ask to see or delete anything you've sent us. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Tidyroll collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. Tidyroll will never start collecting data without saying so clearly first.
